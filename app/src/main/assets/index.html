Model limits: 555 timing uses the standard ⅓–⅔ supply thresholds and ideal pin-7 switching. The CD4017 advances one output on each rising clock edge. Each channel charges through a diode and R1, discharges through R2 and the NPN base, and drives its own LED through R4. The transistor uses a fixed 0.7 V base-emitter drop, an approximate gain β, and a 0.2 V saturation limit. The counter's real output-drive limits and nonlinear transistor curves are not simulated. Brightness and charge-dot speeds are illustrative. An actual short draws fault current; here it is flagged and virtually isolated, not misleadingly modeled as a harmless open. Measure resistance with real circuit power OFF; the R mode only plots nominal component values.
